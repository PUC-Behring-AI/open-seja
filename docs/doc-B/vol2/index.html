Volume 2: The Design Layer

This volume is in draft status. Chapter content has been written; editorial review is pending.

Everything about SEJA's design-intent layer -- the metacommunication message at its apex, the constitution that anchors immutable principles, the design-intent files that evolve with understanding, the marker system that governs what changes and what doesn't, and the UX-research artifacts that ground design in evidence.

Chapters

  1. 1. The Metacommunication Message The one-utterance-of-many-voices message that sits at the apex of the design layer -- how the designer's intent is encoded so the agent can receive it, and why every downstream artifact is a fragment of that single message.
  2. 2. The Constitution The immutable anchor of the design layer. Documents how product-design/constitution.md records principles that do not change, and how those principles constrain everything the agent is allowed to decide.
  3. 3. Design Intent Files The as-intended and as-coded views of design intent -- the living files that evolve as understanding deepens, how the two views are kept in tension, and how drift between them is surfaced and reconciled.
  4. 4. The Marker System The mechanism that governs what changes and what stays fixed. Explains how markers classify content by mutability and how tooling reads them to decide what the agent may safely touch.
  5. 5. UX Research Artifacts The evidence base beneath the design layer. Covers the research artifacts that ground design decisions in observed reality rather than assumption, and how they feed back into design intent.

Recommended Reading Order

Start with "The Metacommunication Message" to understand what the whole layer is communicating, then read "The Constitution" for the immutable ground it rests on. Continue with "Design Intent Files" to see how intent is captured and evolved, then "The Marker System" for the rules that govern change. Finish with "UX Research Artifacts" to see how design stays anchored in evidence.
