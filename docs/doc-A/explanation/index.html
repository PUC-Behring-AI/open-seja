Vol. 4: Explanation -- "Understand SEJA"

Understanding-oriented reading for people who want to know why SEJA is shaped the way it is -- not how to use it, but what ideas it embodies and what commitments those ideas carry. Where the tutorials teach, the how-to guides solve, and the reference specifies, this volume explains: it traces each harness mechanism back to its theoretical foundation and makes the design reasoning visible so you can judge the harness critically, extend it thoughtfully, and contribute to its continued development. These chapters are narrative essays. Read them when you have time to think, not when you are mid-task.

How to Read This Volume

The chapters build on one another and reward being read in order: Chapters 1–3 establish the three theoretical roots (semiotic engineering, the extended metacommunication template, reflective practice); Chapter 4 synthesises them into the unifying thesis; Chapters 5–6 turn the theory back onto the harness itself -- how the agent reads it, and why its structure is what it is. That said, each chapter is self-contained: if one question brought you here, start with the chapter that names it and follow the cross-references outward.

  • New to semiotic engineering? Begin with Ch. 1, then Ch. 2.
  • Curious why SEJA reflects so much? Read Ch. 3.
  • Want the one-paragraph reason SEJA exists? Read Ch. 4.
  • Writing docs or skills for the agent to read? Read Ch. 5.
  • Evaluating a specific design choice? Jump to Ch. 6.
