Vol. 1: Tutorials -- Learn SEJA

Learning-oriented, guaranteed-outcome walkthroughs that build understanding by doing. Each tutorial produces a concrete artifact you can inspect, so you see the result of every step before moving on. Start with Chapter 1 if you have never used SEJA; continue through the remaining chapters to deepen your understanding of the lifecycle, design intent, governance, and epistemic features.

Chapters

  1. Your First SEJA Project -- Clone, set up, and design a working project from scratch using /seja-setup and /design.
  2. The Lifecycle in One Feature -- Walk through /research, /plan, /implement, /critique, and /document by adding a priority field to the to-do app.
  3. Writing Your First Metacommunication Message -- Produce a first-person metacommunication message via the design questionnaire and understand the I/you phrasing convention.
  4. Your First Council Debate -- Run a deep-research council debate to surface disagreement, plural readings, and trade-offs on a high-stakes design question.
  5. Drift Detection and Alignment -- Introduce deliberate drift between design intent and code, then resolve it with /explain drift and the promote workflow.

Reading Guide

The tutorials are designed to be read in order. Chapter 1 establishes the project that Chapters 2-5 build on. However, Chapters 3-5 are independent of each other and can be read in any order after completing Chapters 1 and 2.

Chapter Time Depends on You will learn
Ch. 1: Your First SEJA Project 15 min None Project setup and design intent files
Ch. 2: The Lifecycle in One Feature 20 min Ch. 1 The five-stage development lifecycle
Ch. 3: Metacommunication Message 15 min Ch. 1 First-person design intent and the I/you convention
Ch. 4: Council Debate 15 min Ch. 1 Multi-perspective argumentation for design decisions
Ch. 5: Drift Detection 15 min Ch. 1, Ch. 2 As-intended vs. as-coded alignment and the promote workflow
